# Total NSA (Romania) — website research (construction focus)

Source: https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/ and /en/, crawled 2026-10-16 with curl. 56 pages saved as raw HTML plus extracted .txt in `research/site/`. Sitemaps: `/ro/sitemap.xml`, `/en/sitemap.xml`. The sitemap's `/pagini/*` URLs return 404; the live equivalents are `/ro/despre-noi`, `/ro/contact`, etc.

## Bottom line
- **totalnsa.ro has no construction division section.** The navigation is About / Services / Fleet ("Parc auto") / Blog / Quote / Contact, and all 5 service pages cover transport and logistics.
- Construction appears only as (a) a group company logo, **"Total NSA AG Construct"** (plus **"Top Proiect"**, which may be a design firm; the name suggests it but nothing is stated), and (b) construction-adjacent assets: a concrete batching plant, mixer trucks and pumps, gravel pits/aggregates, an earthmoving fleet and construction tipper trucks.
- The site lists **no construction projects or references**: no clients, locations, years, m² or values, and no GC, subcontractor or design-build role.
- The site states **no certifications**: no ISO 9001/14001/45001, ANRE or ISC. The only accreditation is the RAR-approved ITP (vehicle inspection) station.
- The site names **no software, ERP or BIM tools**. The site was built by MediaServ (mediaserv.ro) in autumn 2020 and looks barely maintained since. The EN version is mostly untranslated or Lorem Ipsum placeholder content, with fake key facts ("3250 / peste 250 / 4 milioane € / 320 500").
- Group activities stated in the 25-year anniversary article (https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/blog/aniversare-a-25-de-ani-de-activitate-total-nsa): road freight is the core business ("obiect principal de activitate transportul rutier de mărfuri"). It is diversified into fuel trading ("comercializarea de carburanti"), mineral aggregates ("agregate minerale") and, over the last 10 years, real estate ("s-a afirmat si pe piata imobiliară").

## Construction service lines (as stated on site)
- **Concrete production and placement ("Cife Beton")**, https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/parc-auto/cife-beton: "Staţia de beton produce beton necesar în diverse construcţii de poduri, case şi orice necesită beton" (concrete for bridges, houses, any concrete works). Plant capacity is **60–80 m³/h**. **7 truck mixers** ("autobetoniere") of 8–10 m³ each. **3 CIFA boom pumps** with 24 m and 28 m booms. On-site **grade II concrete testing laboratory** ("laborator, gradul II"). The fleet summary says "Cife Beton – 5 buc", which conflicts with the 7 mixers stated on the same page.
- **Aggregates / gravel pits ("Balastiere")**: "Balastiere – 7 buc" (https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/parc-auto/). First gravel pit opened 2009 (https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/istoric/). Contact dept "Departament produse balastiera si transport agabaritic", agregatebalastiera@totalnsa.ro. Work point **Balomir** (Hunedoara county, tel. +40 258 760087, balastieraab@totalnsa.ro). "Departamentul de exploatare si transport vrac" reached 95 units in 2015.
- **Earthworks equipment and construction haulage**, https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/parc-auto/basculante: 55 tipping semi-trailers, 40 8x4 trucks, 2 extendable trailers, **20 excavators, 5 bulldozers, 13 front loaders, 4 backhoe loaders**. The transport page lists "Autobasculante specializate pentru transportul in constructii" (https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/servicii/transport-international-si-intern). Bulk cargo includes "agregate de constructii". The 25-year article mentions "un grup propriu de utilaje pentru exploatare". The site does not say whether this equipment is used for contracting or only for quarry operations.
- **Oversize transport ("agabaritic")**, 5 units. Relevant to heavy construction logistics.
- **Not found anywhere on site**: civil works, industrial/logistics warehouse construction, infrastructure or roads, installations (MEP), design-build, building maintenance, prefab/steel structures, design office, construction crews.

## Capabilities (group, construction-relevant)
- Owns heavy plant (see above), a concrete plant with lab, gravel pits, about 100 tippers, and its own workshop.
- Truck workshop "TOTAL NSA TRUCK SERVICE" (https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/servicii/reparatii-si-intretinere): authorised service for VOLVO, IVECO, MERCEDES-BENZ, SCANIA, KRONE trailers and BPW/SAF axles. It also has its own RAR-approved ITP station ("statii ITP, omologată RAR"), ABS/EBS diagnostics and staff trained in the VOLVO technical training system.
- Fuel distribution department (diesel@totalnsa.ro).
- Scale (home page key facts, https://www.totalnsa.ro/ro/): **700 employees, 450+ trucks and special vehicles, €65M turnover, 25M km**. Other pages give lower figures: 450+ employees and 210 trucks on /despre-noi, and 400+ employees, 215 trucks and 80 tippers in the 25-year article.
