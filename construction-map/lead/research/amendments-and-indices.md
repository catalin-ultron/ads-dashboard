Related provisions:
- **art. 222(1)**: any other modification requires a new procedure. **art. 222(2)-(3)**: unilateral termination right, which must be written into the contract.
- **art. 222^1** (OUG 114/2020): the AC publishes in SEAP "toate modificările contractelor ..., astfel încât să rezulte durata şi preţul final".
- **art. 222^2** (Legea 208/2022; amended by OUG 52/2024 in force 24-05-2024): price adjustment by index updates.
  - (1) Adjustment is allowed when price indices for the offer's constituent elements rise or fall.
  - (2)-(3) The adjustment method, the indices and their source ("buletine statistice sau cotaţii ale burselor de mărfuri") must be stated in the tender documents and in the contract.
  - (5) Adjustment only "în măsura strict necesară".
  - (6) It must not change the award outcome.
  - **(8)** The AC "are dreptul" to include adjustment clauses for services/supplies up to 24 months and **works up to 6 months**.
  - **(9)** It "este obligată" to include them for services/supplies over 24 months and **works over 6 months**.
  - (10) Payments are made only at the contractor's own offer prices, fixed or adjusted as the documents provide.
  - (4), (7), (11)-(13) were abrogated by OUG 52/2024.

### 1.4 OUG 64/2022 (EU-funded projects: "fonduri externe nerambursabile")

Scope (art. 1, art. 3): works, related supply contracts, and FS/PT design contracts with geotechnical studies, for **projects financed from EU funds**. For national/own-funds contracts the regime is OUG 47/2022 (section 1.2).

**Unforeseeability conditions, art. 3(13), all cumulative:**
- (a) the economic change occurred after the bid deadline;
- (b) it could not have been foreseen when the offer was prepared. Tested for works against **CNSP-forecast ICC total, Annex 1** (art. 5(1));
- (c) the contractor could not price the risk;
- (d) the contractor proves the cost increase (updated quotes, sworn statement on no foreign state aid).

Art. 3(14): exclusion where delay penalties were applied, or the duration was overrun without an amendment.

**Fixed-price contracts, or contracts whose clause starts only after a period**, use art. 17 until the clause takes effect (art. 3). Under art. 11(4) an existing contract formula may be **replaced** by the a1)/c1) formula with a justification note (art. 43).

- **art. 8, cap.**
  - (1) The price change "se încadrează în prevederile art. 221 alin. (1) lit. c) din Legea nr. 98/2016 ... fără să depăşească **50% din preţul iniţial**".
  - (2) Where there are several successive increases under art. 221(1)(b) and (c), "valoarea cumulată a modificărilor contractului, **inclusiv cele aferente prezentei ordonanţe** ..., nu va depăşi 50% din preţul iniţial". So adjustment, additional works and unforeseen-circumstance modifications **share one 50% budget**.
  - (3) Private beneficiaries (Ordin MFE 1284/2016): also 50%.
- **art. 9, implementation reserve** ("rezervă de implementare"):
  - (1)-(2) The reserve is constituted through an addendum, after the art. 10 steps.
  - **(3)** Initially "în procent de **până la 23% din valoarea restului de executat** ... la momentul intrării în vigoare", usable only for adjustment differences, "fără a depăşi pragurile stabilite la art. 8 şi 18".
  - **(4)** It can be topped up during performance "cu procente de **până la 10% din valoarea restului de executat** ..., la data solicitării", by addendum, within the art. 8/18 caps.
  - **(5)** A top-up is allowed "numai dacă au fost efectuate plăţi de **minimum 75% din valoarea rezervei**".
  - The reserve is kept in separate records and increases the investment cost / technical-economic indicators (regularised under art. 11(6)).
- **art. 10, steps:**
  - Stage I: a justification note on art. 221(1)(c), where meeting art. 3(13) is "necesară şi suficientă", approved by the legal representative.
  - Stage II: an addendum raising the price by the reserve amount. Mandatory content: object; reserve value; records; payments allowed; **adjustment formula**; remaining work by quantity and value at entry into force, up to final acceptance; other provisions; annexes proving unforeseeability.
- **art. 11, timing:**
  - (1) The contractor's request must be made **within 60 days** of entry into force. For contracts signed later from procedures already running, within **30 days of signature**.
  - (3) The addendum is concluded within 30 days of the request.
  - (5) Missing the deadline means losing the right ("decăderea").
  - (6) The final value is set after the last payment request, through a summary statement and an addendum. It must fit the technical-economic indicators, or the indicators are updated.
- **art. 17(1)-(3):** adjustment only of the **remaining value at entry into force**, until works acceptance, **at each payment request** ("la fiecare solicitare de plată").
- **art. 18, procedure-type caps:**
  - (1) For **direct awards**, adjustment "nu trebuie să conducă la o valoare a contractului egală cu sau mai mare decât pragul prevăzut la **art. 7 alin. (5)**" (900,400 lei for works).
  - (2) For **simplified-procedure** contracts, not to or above the **art. 7(1)** threshold (26,960,556 lei for works).
- **Chapter VI art. 30-31 (polynomial formula, for new tenders** in transport, water/wastewater, waste and district heating; OUG 109/2022):
  - Formula: **Vapl = Vpl + (Vsl − Vpl) × Σ_{i=1..10} c_i × ICCr_ci / ICClr_ci**, with Σ c_i = 1. Up to 10 significant cost elements (materials, labour, plant, equipment).
  - ICCr_ci is the INS index "valabil cu 60 de zile înainte de ultima zi din luna ... n". ICClr_ci is the index "valabil cu 30 de zile înainte de data depunerii ofertei".

**art. 17(8) formulas (AFIR consolidated text):**

a1) works in general:
```
Vapl = Vm × [(%av + %p) + (1 − %av − %p) × ICCmr/ICCmlr]
     + (Vpl − Vm)
     + (1 − %cpm) × {(Vpl − Vm) × [(%av + %p) + (1 − %av − %p) × ICCr/ICCplr] − (Vpl − Vm)}
SR (reserve use, a2) = Vapl − Vpl
```
c1) national/EU transport, major water/wastewater, waste (and district heating per OUG 109/2022): the same structure with **ICCr/ICCRlr** (the realised ICC total in the reference month) instead of the forecast ICCplr. The OUG 109/2022 version prints "− (Vapl − Vm)" in the last term. This is probably a typo for (Vpl − Vm): **unverified**.

d1) design services: `Vapl = Vpl × [(%av + %p) + (1 − %av − %p) × IPClr/100]`.

b1) supplies: `Vapl = Vpl × [(%av + %p) + (1 − %av − %p) × IVUITr/IVUITRr]`, with 2022/2023 chain variants using IVUImA2021 and IVUImA2022.

**Term definitions (art. 17(4)-(5), (8)):**
- **Vapl**: the updated value of the payment requested at the date the request is filed.
- **Vpl**: the payment requested at that date (at contract prices).
- **Vm**: the materials cost inside Vpl.
- **ICCmr**: the INS construction cost index for **materials**, realised, "publicat ... în Buletinul Statistic de Preţuri, aplicabil cu **60 de zile înainte de ultima zi a lunii "n"**".
- **ICCmlr**: the INS materials ICC "în luna de referinţă".
- **ICCr**: the INS "indicele de cost în construcţii total", BSP "**tabelul 15**", applicable 60 days before the last day of month n.
- **ICCplr**: the ICC total **forecast by CNSP**, "valabil în luna de referinţă, pentru data cu 60 zile înainte de ultima zi a lunii "n", potrivit **anexei nr. 4**".
- **ICCRlr** (c1 only): the realised ICC total in the reference month.
- **%av**: the advance percentage "valabil la data efectuării plăţii".
- **%p**: the profit percentage in the payment request. If it is absent or cannot be identified, **3%**.
- **%cpm**: the materials weight coefficient used in the ICC total, from **BSP table 15A**, by construction type.
- **Month "n"**: the month the payment request is filed.
- **Reference month**: "luna anterioară faţă de data-limită de depunere a ofertelor". If there was no deadline, 30 days before signature. **A reference month before Jan 2021 is treated as Jan 2021.**

**Month selection (60-day rule):** take the index of the month that contains (last calendar day of n − 60 days). This gives n−1 for a 31-day n preceded by a 30-day month (e.g. Oct 31 − 60 d = Sep 1) and n−2 otherwise (e.g. Mar 31 − 60 d = Jan 30). Implement it literally. Practice guides describe it as "60 days before filing" (devize.ro).

**Provisional indices:**
- "În situaţia în care ... ICCr sau ... ICCmr nu este disponibil şi definitiv, se vor folosi ultimii indici disponibili, iar ajustarea va fi recalculată, pentru diferenţă, în următoarea situaţie de plată, atunci când indicii ... vor fi publicaţi şi definitivi."
- Floors (a1): if ICCr/ICCplr < 1, ICCplr is replaced by the realised ICC total of the reference month. If that ratio is also < 1, it "va avea valoarea 1, până la data de 31.12.2023", subject to art. 8.
- For c1: ratios below 1 reduce the payment, with an optional floor of 1 until 31-12-2023.

- **CNSP does not publish a monthly ICC forecast feed.** Its public macro forecasts (cnp.ro, e.g. spring 2026: https://cnp.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Prognoza-2026-2029-in-profil-teritorial-aferenta-Prognozei-macroeconomice-de-primavara-2026.pdf) contain no ICC series in the results seen. Unverified.

### 1.5 Extra works ("note de comandă suplimentară") pricing and reference month

- **OUG 64/2022 art. 17(8) a1)/c1)**, for works:
  - (a) For extra quantities "care **au echivalent în oferta iniţială**", the reference month is the month before the bid deadline. The original unit prices apply and are adjusted from the original reference month.
  - (b) For quantities "care **nu au echivalent în oferta iniţială**", the reference month is "luna aferentă depunerii ofertei pentru cantităţile suplimentare". New prices are priced at that month.
  - b1) applies the same rule to supplies.
- **OUG 47/2022 art. 3(5)**, national: identical logic. Items with an equivalent use the month before the bid deadline (or the assimilated Jan 2019). Items without one use the month the offer for the extra quantities was submitted.
- **Legea 98 art. 222^2(10)**: payments only on the contractor's own offer prices, fixed or adjusted.
- Whether a "notă de comandă suplimentară" is legally a modification under art. 221(1)(b), (c), (e) or (f) depends on the case. Each one counts toward the art. 221(4) 50% cap (b/c) or the (9) de minimis cumulation (f).
- The general conditions in HG 1/2018 for pricing new items (contract rates, derived rates, or market-based new rates): **not retrieved, unverified**.

## Key numbers (cheat sheet)

- **Art. 221(1)(b) and (c):** each limited to 50% of the initial (updated) value, **cumulatively** across all (b)+(c) changes (art. 221(4)). A JOUE notice is required (art. 221(6)), and every modification goes in SEAP (art. 222^1).
- **Art. 221(1)(f) de minimis:** below 26,960,556 lei **and** below 15% (works) / 10% (supplies and services) of the initial updated price. Only the net cumulative (f) increases are counted (art. 221(9)). The base is the indexed price (art. 221(10)).
- **OUG 64/2022:**
  - 50% of the initial price, shared with art. 221(1)(b)/(c) (art. 8).
  - Reserve of up to 23% of the remaining value, topped up by up to 10% after at least 75% of the reserve has been paid (art. 9).
  - Default profit 3%.
  - Direct-award cap 900,400 lei; simplified-procedure cap 26,960,556 lei (art. 18).
- **OUG 47/2022:** treated as art. 221(1)(e) non-substantial (art. 8), so no 50% cap. Uses ICC total; months before 2019 are assimilated to Jan 2019.
- **ICC feed:** TEMPO CNS107D (2021 = 100). Materials id 23303; total 23295; residential / non-residential / engineering 23299 / 23300 / 23301.
