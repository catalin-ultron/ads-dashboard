## 4. Patterns: project types, contract sizes, client mix

- **Project types:**
  - Energy-efficiency rehabs of schools, kindergartens, colleges and hospitals (mostly PNRR/EU funded).
  - New public buildings: crèche, laboratory, dormitories.
  - Heritage rehab (Maria Tereza).
  - Urban streets and district regeneration (roads, drainage, LED lighting, fibre ducts, bike lanes).
  - County roads and rural roads (Anghel Saligny).
  - Small and medium bridges.
  - Parks and landscaping.
  - Water and sewer networks (with Pro Hart).
  - Many contracts are **design and build** (proiectare + execuție), with in-house or partner designers (ING Proiect Management, Fleschin, Pod-Proiect).
- **Typical contract size:**
  - Core range 5-40M RON (€1-8M).
  - Small street lots 1-5M.
  - Largest single contracts: CL12 water/sewer 83.5M (Pro Hart-led), hospital package ~85M (three contracts), Maria Tereza 39.3M, TURSIB 37.3M, DJ141C 34.9M.
  - Now bidding at the 220M+ level (DJ106E, with Geiger).
- **Public vs private:**
  - The visible pipeline is almost **100% public**: Primăria Sibiu, CJ Sibiu, Apa Canal Sibiu, TURSIB, CNI, ULBS, Politehnica București, communes, CJ Arad, Blaj.
  - No private-client construction projects were found in the press. The only private build seen is the group's own logistics park.
- **Client concentration:** Primăria Municipiului Sibiu is by far the largest client, with ≥14 contracts between 2020 and 2026. Next come CJ Sibiu and Apa Canal Sibiu.
- **Indexation and addenda:** Values typically grow 15-70% through price adjustment (hospital package +70%, TURSIB +19%, DJ141C +19%, crèche +18%).
- **Competition:** Many awards had 1-2 bids (Andrei Șaguna, Veterani, CL12, Politehnica P4, Tilișca park). Others were competitive (crèche 9 bids, Șelimbăr road 10 bids, Grădinița 15 7 bids).
