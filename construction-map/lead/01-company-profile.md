## Operating model

- **Public-works general contractor.** Near 100% public clients; no private projects found. Dominant clients: Sibiu City Hall (≥14 contracts 2020–2026), Sibiu County Council, Apa Canal Sibiu. Expanding since 2025 to Bucharest (Politehnica), Arad County, Blaj.
- **Work mix:** school and hospital rehabilitation/extension (energy efficiency, heritage conversion), street and county roads, bridges, water/sewer networks, laboratories, bus depot, dormitories, crèches, parks.
- **Contract shape:** 5–40 M RON each (€1–8 M), largest ≈ 83.5 M (CL12 water/sewer) and a pending DJ106E bid with works > 220 M. Many design-build ("proiectare și execuție"), funded by EU/PNRR (recovery plan)/Anghel Saligny. Addenda commonly add 15–70% (price adjustment/indexation, extra works). Many awards had 1–2 bidders.
- **Consortia and subcontracting:** recurring partners Pro Hart, John System, ING Proiect Management, Electrocon, Allbizz, Prenis, Prodial/Pod-Proiect, Terra Building, Geiger Transilvania, Dimex, Real Deco. AG Construct acts as leader or partner.
- **Vertical integration:** own quarries and gravel pits (Eurofor, Total NSA Invest; Balomiru de Câmp, Orlat) → own concrete plant 60–80 m³/h with grade II lab, 7 truck mixers 8–10 m³, 3 CIFA pumps 24–28 m → own haulage (80–100 tippers, 40 8x4 trucks) → own earthmoving (20 excavators, 5 bulldozers, 13 loaders, 4 backhoes).
- **Footprint:** Sibiu city and county core; group building a ≈ 28.8 M logistics park at Săliște.
- **Systems:** no ERP, BIM, scheduling or estimating tool found in any public source; no construction job postings; no LinkedIn page.

## Risk and dispute record

- ≈ 26 court cases 2023–2026, mostly procurement disputes (CNSC appeals, contract-execution suits as defendant); civilly liable party in a 2026 criminal case (art. 296).
- Sibiu county hospital contracts (2022): large delays, +70% indexation, no penalties applied; press allegations of political ties (denied, not findings).
- Maria Tereza school conversion (2026, 39.3 M): award challenged at CNSC by Euras; contract signed.
- Săliște: Court of Accounts damage 2.95 M over a 27 ha land sale; payment disputed.
- 2019–2021 tax/anti-fraud probes on the logistics parent (aggregates for A1 motorway); one audit referred to DIICOT.
- No insolvency, no state-budget debt at 31.12.2025, VAT active.

## Operational activities implied

- Tender pipeline: SEAP/SICAP monitoring, DUAE, bid pricing, consortium agreements, guarantees (participation, good-execution), CNSC complaints.
- Design-build: managing subcontracted design phases (DALI, PT, DE), technical verifiers, ISC permits and notifications.
- Contract execution: works programmes, monthly "situații de lucrări" (measured progress billing) against bill of quantities, addenda and price adjustment formulas, retention via good-execution guarantee, reception (la terminarea lucrărilor, finală), "cartea tehnică a construcției".
- Site operations: daily site reports, site supervision interface (diriginte de șantier), quality control and testing (own lab), H&S coordination, waste tracking.
- Supply chain: internal transfers of aggregates, concrete and haulage between group companies; plant and fleet scheduling; fuel; maintenance.
- Subcontractor management: subcontract packages, back-to-back billing, payments, declarations to the contracting authority.
- Finance and compliance: project cost control per contract and lot, cash flow on EU/PNRR reimbursement cycles, e-Factura, SAF-T (D406), payroll and Revisal for ≈ 150 staff, intra-group invoicing.
- Asset base: heavy equipment fleet, quarries (mining licences, ANRM royalties), concrete plant.
